The House

A house with history.
A place to feel at home.

Casa Colletti is a 300-year-old holiday home in Tuscany, rented as a whole to one group of up to 8 guests. We are looking after it with a light touch, keeping as much of its original character as possible. Old timber beams, antique furniture and the gentle wear of generations are all part of what makes it special.

The Mountains

Lace up
and head out.

From the house, trails lead straight into the Apuan Alps (Alpi Apuane), the marble mountains of northern Tuscany. Take a picnic and spend the day exploring woodland paths, neighbouring villages and open views towards the sea.

  • Leave the car behind: start your walk at the front door
  • Follow historic mule trails: wander over to the neighbouring village of Metato
  • Walk to Grotta all’Onda: discover the cave on the slopes of Monte Matanna
  • Head for higher ground: take mountain trails with far-reaching coastal views
  • Find a place to cool off: walk down to the waterfalls and natural pools of Candalla

Climbing

Days at the crag.
Evenings on the terrace.

The Camaiorese climbing area, with more than 50 limestone crags near Camaiore, surrounds Casoli, making the house a welcoming base for a climbing trip with friends.

  • Explore nearby crags: including Cannelot, Pilastri di Setriana and San Rocchino
  • Walk to some of the climbing spots: with approaches starting from the village
  • Stay together: space for your friends and climbing gear
  • Unwind afterwards: make dinner together and linger on the terrace

The Beach

A little mountain air.
A little salt water.

The sea is part of the view from the terrace, and close enough to be part of your day. The Versilia coast is around a 30-minute drive from the village car park.

  • Lido di Camaiore: sandy beaches, a pier and leisurely walks along the seafront
  • Viareggio: a swim followed by a stroll along the promenade
  • Lecciona: a free-access natural beach backed by dunes and pine woods
  • No need to choose between mountains and sea: make time for both

The Village

Start the day
with a cappuccino.

Casoli is a small, car-free village above Camaiore in Tuscany, and life here unfolds along narrow lanes and stone steps. Pictures etched into the plaster decorate the houses, and the village café is a lovely place to pause.

  • Breakfast with a view: a cappuccino, a cornetto and a warm welcome at the panoramic village café
  • Discover the sgraffiti: artworks carved into the plaster of the village façades
  • Take your time exploring: little passageways, old stairways and unexpected views
  • Stay where the village meets the mountains: at the upper edge of Casoli, where the walking trails begin

FAQ

Questions about Casa Colletti.

How do I get to the house?
Casa Colletti sits in the car-free heart of Casoli, and that is a big part of its charm. You park for free about 350m below the house and walk up around 160 steps, roughly 10 minutes. Luggage and groceries are carried up by hand, so a backpack is easier than a big suitcase. Because of the steps, the house is unfortunately not suitable for guests with limited mobility.
Can I hike straight from the house?
Yes! A trail starts right at the front door and takes you into the Apuan Alps nature park. You can leave the car where it is for the whole day.
Is there a sea view, and how far is the beach?
Yes, from the terrace you look out over the Versilia coast to the sea. Lido di Camaiore is about 30 minutes by car, Forte dei Marmi and Viareggio about 35 minutes.
How many people can stay?
Up to 8 guests: three bedrooms with eight beds, 140m² on two floors, one bathroom with shower and bathtub plus a separate WC. The whole house, both terraces and the garden are just for you.
When is the best time to visit?
Casa Colletti is lovely all year round. Spring (April to early June) and autumn (September and October) are perfect for hiking, with mild days and clear views. In summer the beach is calling, and in winter the fireplace makes the house cosy.
Is it suitable for children?
Children are very welcome! If they can manage the 160 steps on their own, they will love the village and the trails. A pushchair cannot be taken up to the house, so a baby carrier is the better choice. There is no pool, but the natural pools in the Candalla valley are a wonderful alternative in summer.
Can I work from the house?
Yes. The WiFi is reliable, and there is a workspace with two monitors. Work in the morning, walk into the mountains in the afternoon.
What are check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is from 12:00, check-out until 16:00. We send you all the details for your arrival after booking. If you would like to arrive earlier or leave later, just ask and we will do our best.
What is the cancellation policy?
We know plans can change. If you cancel 14 days or more before arrival, you get a full refund. Between 14 and 7 days before arrival we charge 30% of the booking. Cancellations less than 7 days before arrival cannot be refunded.
How do I book?
Just write to us at colletti.casoli@gmail.com with your dates, or book via Airbnb. The minimum stay is 3 nights. We usually reply within a day.
Where exactly is Casa Colletti?
Casa Colletti is a holiday house in Casoli, a hill village in the municipality of Camaiore (province of Lucca, Tuscany), at 500m above the Versilia coast. The beach at Lido di Camaiore is about 30 minutes by car, Lucca about 45 minutes and Pisa about 50 minutes.
Is Casa Colletti a good base for hiking and climbing in the Versilia?
Yes. Trails into the Apuan Alps start at the front door, and the house sits in the middle of the Camaiorese climbing area with crags such as Cannelot, Pilastro di Setriana and San Rocchino. Some of them can be reached on foot from the village.
Which airport is closest?
Pisa airport is the closest, about 50 minutes by car. Florence airport is about 1 hour 30 minutes away. A car is useful for day trips, and you leave it at the free car park below the village.
